GPTblox

GPTblox was a roblox revival i started working on in August of 2025. it started as a 2008 revival and wasnt really doing anywhere until a guy named variio joined in to help.
By October 2025 we had finished a good part of the website and we decided to make a poll if we should switch to 2013. That poll ended up winning and we started to work on the 2013 version of gptblox.
The project was going well and by november we had revamped most of the website to look 2013. we had a growing community. but by mid november we shut the project down adue to there being a lot of vulerabilities in the code.
